# Higgsfield prompts for the remaining renders

Workflow tips for consistency:
1. Attach the REAL aerial photo of Ohio Stadium as the primary reference
   image, and your liked district generation as a secondary style reference.
2. Generate wide (16:9 minimum; 21:9 for the hero), then export ~2560px wide
   as JPG at ~80% quality and save under the exact filenames noted.
3. Reuse the shared style block at the bottom verbatim at the end of each
   prompt so all renders share one golden-hour palette.
