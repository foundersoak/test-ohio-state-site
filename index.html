The Master Plan

Read the full precinct

Precinct 01

The Amphitheater

A 4,300-seat open-air venue under a white tensile roof, with the Horseshoe itself as the backdrop. Concerts, commencements, watch parties, and community events, programmed year-round to anchor the district's nightlife and give Columbus a stage it doesn't have.

Athletics upside: ticketing share · naming rights · sponsorship · food and beverage

Precinct 02

The Scarlet Spine

A pedestrian retail promenade running from campus to the stadium gates: restaurants, flagship retail, and Buckeye-first storefronts that turn the walk to the game into the destination itself.

Athletics upside: retail leases · flagship team store · brand activations

Precinct 03

Buckeye Tower

A full-service hotel and conference venue overlooking the stadium, serving recruits' families, visiting teams, alumni weekends, and the university's year-round events calendar.

Athletics upside: ground lease participation · recruiting hospitality · naming partner

Precinct 04

The Riverfront

The Olentangy edge, opened up: a landscaped promenade, trail connections, and terraced public space that ties the district to the water and to the campus beyond it.

Civic upside: public space · trail connections · land value uplift

Precinct 05

Residential Towers

Market-rate and graduate residences with stadium views: a built-in population that keeps the district’s restaurants, shops, and riverfront busy every day of the year.

University upside: ground lease income · year-round district population
